# Phase 2.5 — Analytical Method Selection

> This document records the analytical reasoning used to determine whether advanced analytical methods are required after completing **Phase 2 — Business Analysis**.
>
> The purpose of this phase is **not to build models**, but to ensure that any analytical method selected is appropriate for the business question, supported by the available evidence, and justified according to the principles of the Data Analysis Playbook.
>
> Always select the **simplest method capable of answering the business question**.
